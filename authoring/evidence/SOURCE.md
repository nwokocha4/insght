# Source provenance

The graded values in `tests/expected.json` are transcribed from the following open-access article. The canonical PDF is stored beside this note as `zhao2026_camera_trap_diversity.pdf` and copied verbatim into `environment/data/reference/` and `tests/reference/`.

Zhao W, Li M-M, Yan X-R, Zhang Z, Chen X-C, Chang Z-Q, Chen J-L, Ma J-J, Xu Z-P, Chen J-M, Lee PS, Zhang F (2026). Camera trap survey of mammal diversity and activity rhythms of threatened species in a subtropical forest of Huangshan Mountain, China. *Biodiversity Data Journal* 14: e184017. DOI 10.3897/BDJ.14.e184017. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

Graded transcriptions:

- Table 2, low altitude (400–780 m): 16 cameras, Shannon-Weiner 1.610, Pielou 0.594, Simpson 0.309.
- Table 2, mid-altitude (780–1,210 m): 16 cameras, Shannon-Weiner 1.687, Pielou 0.639, Simpson 0.259.
- Table 1, Reeves's Muntjac: 3,480 independent photos.
- Table 1, Wild Boar: 1,420 independent photos.
- Cover page: title, DOI, publication year 2026, journal *Biodiversity Data Journal*.
